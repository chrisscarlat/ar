AR Artworks

Point your phone at a painting and watch it come to life. Scan the QR code next to an artwork to start. No app needed: it runs in Safari and Chrome.

How it works

  1. Scan the QR code next to the artwork with your phone camera.
  2. Tap Start and allow camera access.
  3. Hold your phone so the whole painting is in view.

For artists and galleries

Add artworks, upload videos and download QR codes in the admin area.
